01

One source of truth

The largest architectural change is not a probability tweak. It is deciding that downstream systems consume one canonical specimen instead of reconstructing state independently.

Legacy

State could be derived in several places

  • Tide selection, size, minigame inputs, Tideborne trait helpers, compatibility components, UI, and records evolved separately.
  • Old compatibility logic could mirror or reconstruct fields with slightly different assumptions.
  • Several concepts used the word “luck” while changing different parts of the catch.
Fishing System 2.0

SpecimenData is canonical

  • Species, deterministic seed, natural percentile, base and final length, all four trait axes, Perfect Catch, raw score, final score, and provenance live together.
  • Satchel, Journal, records, history, teams, networking, and tooltips consume that state.
  • No downstream system is allowed to reroll specimen identity.

02

Fishing Luck gets one job

Legacy luck touched overlapping loot behavior. V2 Fishing Luck only changes relative rarity inside the eligible fish pool.

Legacy

Luck was overloaded

Luck could interact with fish weight, treasure, junk, or per-entry selection behavior. Compatibility fish did not always share equivalent semantics.

Fishing System 2.0

Rarity-aware fish weighting

W' = W × (1 + Cᵣ × √max(L, 0))

Fishing Luck changes relative fish rarity, not the overall probability that the catch is a fish.

Interactive Fishing Luck

At +15: the target multipliers are approximately 1.00×, 1.31×, 1.62×, 1.93×, and 2.24× from 1★ through 5★.

03

Keep the minigame, replace the math feeding it

The marker, catch region, behavior pattern, and Perfect Catch center zone stay. V2 normalizes legacy and compatibility values before the minigame sees them.

Legacy Speed

Different source values could collapse together

A hard floor meant several nominally different fish speeds could produce effectively similar movement.

effective speed ≈ max(source speed, floor)
V2 Tempo

Smooth logarithmic normalization

V = 0.04 + 0.085 × ln(1+s) / ln(3.2)

Useful differences remain visible from very slow fish through high-speed outliers.

Strength also becomes a normalized fight input

A = 0.78 − 0.58 × S^1.25

The target is simple: Strength 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, and 1.0 should feel meaningfully different, while external values are normalized before reaching the model.

04

One natural percentile, one physical specimen

V2 keeps useful Tide species-size distributions but replaces sampled percentile approximation with direct distribution math.

Legacy

Percentile approximation and tail-gated logic

  • Percentile helpers could rely on large finite samples.
  • Other systems could use thresholds such as extreme tails to unlock size traits.
  • Physical modifiers and trait logic were harder to reason about as one specimen story.
Fishing System 2.0

Canonical size state

basePercentile → baseLength → physical modifiers → finalLength → direct CDF → finalPercentile

finalPercentile is size-adjusted, deterministic, and never a second random percentile roll.

Size effect on the fight

Species stays dominant. Size adds a mild, predictable feel shift.

05

Mutations become independent trait axes

This is the major collection-system redesign. Individual traits can be reasonably obtainable without making multi-trait trophy specimens common.

Legacy

Special states competed in a mutation-style system

Rare visual and specimen states were more tightly coupled, so increasing one chance could distort the rarity of every other special result.

Fishing System 2.0

Four independent axes

Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation, and Specimen Quality are separately generated and can coexist.

Why rarity compensation matters

A rare species still takes longer to find, but once you finally catch one, its special-trait odds are intentionally better so species rarity does not simply multiply trait rarity into absurd grinds.

Trait Momentum: bounded bad-luck protection

Momentum is hidden, server-authoritative, and per species. A fully normal catch adds roughly +1 temporary Trait Luck, capped around +15. A notable trait substantially reduces Momentum. There is no hard guarantee at catch N, and a first-catch special specimen remains possible.

06

Giant and Dwarf become Body Types

No more “extreme percentile then special state” identity. The Body Type event is separate, with percentile only biasing which subtype is more likely.

Legacy

Tail gates

Giant and Dwarf were associated with hard extreme-percentile gates in legacy behavior, making them a compound rarity problem.

Fishing System 2.0

Smooth subtype bias

The Body Type event begins at 5%. Once triggered, natural percentile smoothly biases Giant versus Dwarf, but neither subtype becomes impossible.

07

Perfect Catch becomes skill-driven trait pressure

Skill should improve the odds of an exceptional specimen without rewriting the specimen's natural size after the fight.

Legacy

Perfect Catch could influence specimen outcomes through older compatibility logic

The redesign specifically removes any need for Perfect Catch to alter natural size or percentile after the fight.

Fishing System 2.0

Skill bonus, not specimen rewrite

  • Keep the center-zone skill check
  • +10 temporary Trait Luck
  • Body Type event chance ×1.25
  • Substantial Perfect Specimen bonus
  • No natural size reroll

Perfect Specimen base curve

Below P95, the base chance is zero. Above that, the curve rises sharply toward truly exceptional natural specimens. Trait Luck and Perfect Catch are applied after this base curve.

08

FishScore becomes explainable and stable

V2 separates specimen value from record ownership and uses a deterministic raw score before linear normalization to the public 1 through 3000 scale.

Legacy

Score logic evolved alongside records and specimen mechanics

Legacy score numbers are not preserved during V2 migration. The specimen identity is preserved, then every score is recalculated under one system.

Fishing System 2.0

Three-part raw score

RawScore = SpeciesValue + (3 × finalPercentile) + TraitValue

Then one frozen linear mapping converts valid raw scores to 1 through 3000.

Why records are separate

A fish's score should never change because another player later catches a larger record. Largest, Smallest, Highest FishScore, Best Percentile, and notable trait-combination records remain separate ranking systems.
