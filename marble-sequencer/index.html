Multitrack MIDI editor

Edit the actual imported MIDI tracks and individual notes. Unlike 16-step quantization, original timing, channels, and track separation are retained. Export retains the imported tempo map for standard PPQ MIDI files; unsupported timing formats and legacy JSON fall back to 120 BPM.

Piano roll

Click or tap a note to select it. Drag a note to change pitch and timing, or drag its right edge to resize. A drag saves as one undo step.

Supported files

MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, Ogg, Opus, AIFF, WMA, and other decodable audio. Standard MIDI (.mid, .midi), RMID, and KAR files play through an in-browser synth.

Your files stay on your device. Uncommon audio formats may download an extended decoder.
