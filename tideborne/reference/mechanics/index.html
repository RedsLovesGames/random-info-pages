Fishing Luck

Fishing Luck = 1 + (# vanilla Lure enchants × 2)

Fishing Luck changes species weights. It does not directly roll specimen traits.

Trait Luck

score = int(5 × ConfigScale)
chanceMultiplier = max(0, 1 + 0.10 × score)
thresholdScale = 1 / chanceMultiplier

Specimen traits

The base notable-trait event is about 5% before modifiers. Condition and Pigmentation split their rare outcomes using a ±0.005 threshold around the center of the rare event. Perfect Specimen uses a high-percentile curve near the top of the specimen range.

Perfect Catch streak

bonus = streakBefore + 1
streakAfter = min(streakBefore + 1, 12)

A non-Perfect result resets the Perfect Catch streak to 0.

Trait Momentum

Trait Momentum is per player and per species. It gives bad-luck protection after repeated misses. Numeric cap and decay values are not published here until they are re-verified against the current runtime code.

FishScore

The final score is mapped onto a 1–3000 scale.

normalized = (raw - min) / (max - min)
FishScore = 1 + 2999 × sqrt(clamp(normalized, 0, 1))
