Tideborne/Fishing/Condition
Specimen axis

Condition tells part of the fish's story.

Condition rolls independently from Body Type, Pigmentation and Quality. Most catches are Normal; a notable condition event produces Scarred or Parasite-Ridden.

Base notable event5%
Scarred split65%
Parasite-Ridden split35%
Stacks with other traitsYes

How the roll works

The 5% value is the starting event chance before rarity compensation, Trait Luck and other applicable modifiers. Only after the notable condition event succeeds is the result split between Scarred and Parasite-Ridden.

Why the subtype matters

Both notable states contribute to specimen distinction and FishScore, but they do not carry identical trait points. The score reference shows the exact values.

Independent means independentA Scarred Giant is valid. So is a Parasite-Ridden Albino Perfect Specimen. Condition does not occupy a single shared “rare trait slot.”
