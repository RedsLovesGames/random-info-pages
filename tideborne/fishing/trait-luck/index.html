Fishing

Trait Luck helps special traits appear.

Trait Luck does not choose the fish species. It changes the chance that the fish gets special specimen traits.

What it changes

More Trait Luck makes special Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation, and Quality results easier to reach.

Rare fish get help too

Rare fish get a bonus when Trait Luck is calculated. This helps rare species still have a fair chance to become special specimens.

Fishing Luck is different

Fishing Luck

Changes which species you catch.

Trait Luck

Changes how special that caught fish can be.
