Tideborne/Fishing/Trait Luck
Specimen hunting

Fishing Luck finds species. Trait Luck finds strange fish.

Trait Luck is Tideborne's lever for unusual specimen traits. Keeping it separate from Fishing Luck prevents “rare species” and “rare specimen” from becoming the same stat.

Fishing Luck

Changes relative species-selection weights inside the currently eligible fish pool.

Trait Luck

Improves the applicable specimen-trait chances after the species and natural specimen foundation exist.

Where Trait Luck can come from

Trait Luck can include the sources active for that catch, including gear/configured effects, per-species Trait Momentum and the temporary bonus granted by a Perfect Catch.

Rare species receive standard-trait compensation

Body Type, Condition and Pigmentation use species-rarity compensation before Trait Luck. The multiplier grows from 1★ through 5★ so rare species are not punished as harshly by stacking a rare-species roll on top of rare-trait rolls.

1★ ×1.002★ ×1.153★ ×1.404★ ×1.805★ ×2.40

Quality is the exception

Perfect Specimen does not use rarity compensation. It uses its percentile curve, then Trait Luck, then the dedicated Perfect Catch quality reward if applicable.
