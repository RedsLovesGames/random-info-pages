Tideborne/Fishing
Fishing system

From catch to specimen.

Tide 2 catches the fish. Tideborne gives that catch its own size, traits, score, and records.

The five steps

1. Species

Tide 2 picks the fish.

2. Size

The fish gets its own size.

3. Traits

Special traits may appear.

4. FishScore

The full catch gets a score.

5. Records

Keep and compare your best fish.

Two kinds of luck

Fishing Luck

Changes which fish you are more likely to catch.

Trait Luck

Changes the chance of special traits on the fish you already caught.

Trait groups

Body Type

Normal, Giant, or Dwarf.

Condition

Normal, Scarred, or Parasite-Ridden.

Pigmentation

Normal, Albino, or Iridescent.

Quality

Includes Perfect Specimen.

Skill and bad luck

Perfect Catch rewards good timing. Trait Momentum helps after repeated bad luck.
