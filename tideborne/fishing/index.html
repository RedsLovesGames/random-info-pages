Tideborne/Fishing
Core system

Fishing System 2.0

The central idea is simple: which species you hook and what makes that individual fish special are separate systems.

Two kinds of luckFishing Luck affects species selection. Trait Luck affects unusual specimen traits. More Fishing Luck does not directly make a fish Giant, Albino, Scarred or Perfect.

One catch, in player terms

1 · SpeciesThe eligible pool is built and Fishing Luck changes relative weights.
2 · Natural specimenNatural size information and percentile are established.
3 · FightTide's catch minigame resolves player skill.
4 · Perfect CatchThe center-zone skill result is known before final trait odds settle.
5 · TraitsRarity, Trait Luck, Momentum and Perfect Catch apply where defined.
6 · FinalizeBody, condition, pigmentation, quality and size become final.
7 · FishScoreThe canonical score is calculated.
8 · PersistThe same specimen identity follows the trophy through Tideborne systems.

Four independent specimen axes

The standard trait categories are intentionally independent. A Giant specimen can also be Scarred, Iridescent and Perfect if those separate rolls resolve that way.

Body Type

Normal, Giant or Dwarf. The unusual-body event and its Giant/Dwarf subtype are separate decisions.

Condition

Normal, Scarred or Parasite-Ridden.

Pigmentation

Normal, Albino or Iridescent.

Quality

Perfect Specimen uses a dedicated high-percentile probability curve.

Skill still matters

Perfect Catch rewards hitting Tide's center catch zone. It does not reroll the species you hooked or replace the natural specimen percentile; it improves defined trait opportunities after the fight.

Bad-luck protection is species-specific

Trait Momentum belongs to the player and species. Repeated fully-normal catches build future Trait Luck for that species; a notable trait result resets that species' Momentum.

Everything can be compared without becoming identical

FishScore maps species value, specimen percentile and compatible trait points to 1–3000. The score is a comparison tool; the specimen's actual traits and persistent identity remain the trophy.
