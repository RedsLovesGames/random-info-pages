Tideborne/Fishing/FishScore
Fishing

One number for the whole catch.

FishScore lets you compare specimens on a 1–3000 scale.

What raises FishScore

The score uses the fish's species value, size, percentile, and supported special traits. A strong combination makes a stronger score.

Species

Some species have more score potential.

Size

Exceptional size can add value.

Traits

Rare Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation, and Quality can add value.

Range

The final scale runs from 1 to 3000.

What it is for

Use FishScore for records, Top Fish, and quick comparisons. Keep the trait labels too: they explain why a fish is special.
