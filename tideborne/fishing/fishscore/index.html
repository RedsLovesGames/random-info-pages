One scale. The specimen still matters.

FishScore V2 maps the meaningful score inputs onto a linear 1–3000 scale so trophies can be ranked without flattening their actual traits into a generic rarity label.

What contributes

The raw score combines species points, three times specimen percentile, and the compatible trait points. The raw range is then mapped linearly to 1–3000.

Species

Higher star tiers contribute more base species points.

Percentile

Specimen percentile has a direct continuous contribution.

Traits

Scarred, Parasite-Ridden, Giant, Dwarf, Albino, Iridescent and Perfect Specimen each add defined points.

Scale

The final mapping is linear from the canonical raw minimum to maximum.

Why not just show rarity?

Two fish of the same species can have very different specimen percentiles and trait combinations. FishScore gives the Journal, records and players one comparable number while the trait labels still explain why one fish scored higher.
