Tideborne/Fishing/Trait Momentum
Fishing

Bad luck should not last forever.

Trait Momentum is bad-luck protection for special specimen traits.

How it works

If one species keeps missing special traits, Momentum helps future catches of that same species.

Momentum is tracked for each player and each species. Catching one species does not build Momentum for every other species.

When you get a special catch

A meaningful special result ends or reduces the bad-luck streak.

Why it exists

Very unlucky streaks can feel unfair. Momentum keeps rare specimen hunting difficult without letting bad luck continue forever.
