Tideborne/Fishing/Trait Momentum
Bad-luck protection

The more ordinary catches, the more pressure builds.

Trait Momentum is persistent bad-luck protection tracked per player and per species. A dry streak on one fish helps future catches of that fish without globally inflating every species.

ScopePlayer + species
Fully normal catch+1 Momentum
Notable specimenReset to 0
Cap15

What builds Momentum

A fully normal specimen increases that species' Momentum for that player. The accumulated value contributes temporary Trait Luck on future catches of the same species.

What resets it

A notable result on Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation or Quality resets that species' Momentum to zero. The system rewards the drought and then clears the pressure once the player gets an unusual specimen.

Why it is server-owned

Because Momentum affects future specimen generation and persists across catches, Tideborne keeps the authoritative state on the server instead of letting a client predict or manufacture it.

ExampleA long run of ordinary Salmon can build Salmon Momentum without giving the same accumulated bonus to every other species you catch.
