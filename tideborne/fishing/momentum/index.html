Tideborne/Fishing/Trait Momentum
Bad-luck protection

The more ordinary catches, the more pressure builds.

Trait Momentum is optional persistent bad-luck protection tracked per player and per species. A dry streak on one fish helps future catches of that fish without globally inflating every species.

What builds Momentum

The frozen Fishing System 2.0 design describes a fully normal specimen as adding roughly one Momentum for that species and player. The accumulated value contributes temporary Trait Luck on future catches of that same species.

What a notable catch does

A notable result on Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation or Quality substantially reduces that species' accumulated Momentum. The current frozen specification intentionally does not define this as a universal unconditional reset-to-zero rule, so this player page does not pretend otherwise.

Why it is server-owned

Because Momentum affects future specimen generation and persists across catches, Tideborne keeps the authoritative state on the server instead of letting a client predict or manufacture it.

Species-specificA long run of ordinary Salmon can build Salmon Momentum without granting the same accumulated bonus to every other species you catch.
