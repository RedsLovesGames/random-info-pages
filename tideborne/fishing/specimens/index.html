Tideborne/Fishing/Specimens
Fishing

One fish, one specimen.

A specimen is one fish you caught. Its size and traits stay with that fish.

What a specimen has

Species

What kind of fish it is.

Size

How large that fish is.

Traits

Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation, and Quality.

FishScore

A score used to compare the whole catch.

The fish keeps its information

Moving a fish into the Satchel or opening the Journal does not make a new fish. The same specimen keeps the same size and traits.

Why this matters

Two fish of the same species can still be very different. They can have different sizes, traits, and FishScores.
