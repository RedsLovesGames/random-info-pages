Tideborne/Fishing/Specimens
Fishing 2.0

A fish is more than its species.

A Tideborne specimen is the persistent identity of one catch. Species tells you what fish it is; the specimen records what made this individual catch different.

What belongs to a specimen

Natural size

The catch receives natural size information and a specimen percentile.

Body Type

Normal, Giant or Dwarf.

Condition

Normal, Scarred or Parasite-Ridden.

Pigmentation

Normal, Albino or Iridescent.

Quality

Normal or Perfect Specimen.

FishScore

A canonical 1–3000 comparison score derived from compatible specimen information.

Rare traits can stack

Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation and Quality are independent axes. A notable result on one axis does not consume the chance of another axis being notable on the same catch.

GiantScarredIridescentPerfect Specimen
That combination is legal.

The point of the independent model is to allow memorable combinations rather than forcing every specimen into one mutually-exclusive rarity state.

The identity follows the trophy

Tideborne treats specimen generation as server-authoritative and preserves the same canonical specimen through its supported item/entity/storage/Journal paths instead of rerolling traits when a fish changes presentation.

Why this mattersIf a fish is special, the system is designed so that specialness belongs to the specimen—not to whichever screen happens to be showing it.
