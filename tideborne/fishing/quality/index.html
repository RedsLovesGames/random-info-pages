Specimen axis

Perfect Specimen rewards exceptional percentiles.

Quality is intentionally different from the standard Body Type, Condition and Pigmentation events. Perfect Specimen uses a dedicated percentile curve instead of rarity compensation.

Values between the anchors interpolate linearly. Trait Luck then improves the resulting chance.

Species rarity does not compensate Quality

Rare species get compensation for the standard notable trait events, but Perfect Specimen deliberately does not use that rarity multiplier. Quality stays tied to specimen percentile, Trait Luck and the defined Perfect Catch reward.

Perfect Catch gets a dedicated reward

After Trait Luck is applied, a Perfect Catch receives the defined second-opportunity quality treatment. It improves the chance without rerolling species or the natural specimen percentile.
