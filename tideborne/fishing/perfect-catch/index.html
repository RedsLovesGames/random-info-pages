Tideborne/Fishing/Perfect Catch
Skill reward

Hit the center. Improve the specimen opportunity.

Perfect Catch is Tideborne's reward for Tide's center-zone skill check. It is determined during the fight, then affects defined trait opportunities without replacing the fish you already hooked.

Temporary Trait Luck+10

What Perfect Catch does

It contributes +10 temporary Trait Luck for that catch, multiplies the Body Type event chance by 1.25 before the later standard modifiers, and gives Perfect Specimen its dedicated quality second opportunity.

What it does not do

Perfect Catch does not reroll species and does not reroll the natural specimen percentile. Skill improves the finished specimen opportunity rather than secretly replacing the fish that was already selected.

Design intentThe minigame matters, but it does not turn every excellent input into the same guaranteed trophy. Skill improves the odds while specimen hunting remains a collection game.
