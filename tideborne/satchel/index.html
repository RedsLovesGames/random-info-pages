Tideborne/Angler's Satchel
Storage

Keep the fish that matter.

The Angler's Satchel stores specimens and helps you protect, sort, and review them.

Four tabs

Contents

See stored fish and open one fish's details.

Sorting

Choose how stored fish are ordered.

Upgrades

Unlock more Satchel features and capacity.

Records

Review your stored record fish.

Active Satchel

You can choose which Satchel is active. The active Satchel is used by features that need your current Satchel.

Protect a fish

Protection helps stop an important specimen from being removed by normal Satchel actions.

Capacity

The Satchel can hold up to 192 specimens when fully expanded.

Simple ruleThe Satchel does not make a new fish. A stored specimen keeps the same size and traits.
