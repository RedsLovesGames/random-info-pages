Tide Journal vs Tideborne records

Tide foundationDiscovery, habitats & fishing information

The base Journal helps players understand what they have caught and where fish belong.

Tideborne layerPersistent specimens, score & multiplayer records

Tideborne keeps individual specimen identity meaningful after the initial discovery.

Specimen history

Individual catches can carry persistent size and trait information. Journal presentation reads that canonical data rather than recalculating a different client-side specimen.

Records & Top Fish

Personal records and Top Fish let exceptional catches stand out. FishScore provides a sortable comparison scale while trait labels preserve why a catch was unusual.

Teams

Team records extend the same record concept to multiplayer. Tideborne's architecture keeps Journal/history/team/Top Fish identities and network behavior compatible across the P1–P10 cleanup.
