Equipment

Build around the catch you want.

Tide already gives rods an equipment language. Tideborne extends that language with specimen hunting, catch-loss protection and Tideborne-specific interactions while preserving Tide-owned gear behavior.

Gear categories

Lines

Change catch handling and related fishing behavior.

Leaders

Trade handling against catch-loss protection. Tideborne keeps their historical registry IDs compatible even though the player-facing presentation is cleaner.

Bobbers

Tideborne understands all supported Tide bobber variants and can surface registered effects without duplicating Tide's own tooltip presentation.

Bait & hooks

Modify catch selection, tempo, luck or other defined fishing behavior depending on the item.

Read tradeoffs, not config variables

The final gear database will present effects in player terms—catch window, fish speed, protection, luck and special behavior—then expose exact multipliers underneath for players who want them.

P10 presentation ruleVanilla advanced tooltip information such as registry IDs is not globally suppressed. Tideborne adds useful effect information while leaving Minecraft's F3+H diagnostics available.
