Tideborne/Gear
Equipment

Build around the catch you want.

Tide already gives rods an equipment language. Tideborne extends that language with specimen hunting, catch-loss protection and Tideborne-specific interactions while preserving Tide-owned gear behavior.

The official Tide wiki includes useful renders, sprite sheets and Angling Table screenshots. Those visuals are incorporated here while current Tideborne code/reference data remains authoritative for effects and values.

Read tradeoffs, not config variables

The generated gear database will present effects in player terms—catch window, fish speed, protection, luck and special behavior—then expose exact multipliers underneath for players who want them.

P10 presentation ruleVanilla advanced tooltip information such as registry IDs is not globally suppressed. Tideborne adds useful effect information while leaving Minecraft's F3+H diagnostics available.
Upstream version noteThe older Tide wiki may describe an accessory differently from current Tide/Tideborne behavior. Use this page's future generated stats and the exact mechanics layer for current values; use the upstream media for visual identification.
