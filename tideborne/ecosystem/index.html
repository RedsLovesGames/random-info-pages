Tideborne/Sharks & Ecosystem
Fishing risk

The water is part of the system.

Tideborne's ecosystem features connect sharks, chum, shark scent and catch-loss behavior to fishing without making those systems look like unrelated compatibility code.

Sharks

Shark behavior belongs to Tideborne's ecosystem layer. Optional spawning controls let packs or servers choose how much of that layer they want active.

Chum & scent

Chum and shark-scent behavior are player-facing ecosystem mechanics. Exact recipes, durations and configured values will be generated from authoritative data in the reference phase rather than guessed here.

Catch loss

Catch-loss pressure is where leaders become meaningful: different protection levels can trade easier handling for a better chance of preserving a valuable catch.
