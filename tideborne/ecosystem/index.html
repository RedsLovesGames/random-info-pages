Tideborne/Sharks & Ecosystem
Ecosystem

The water can fight back.

Tideborne can add shark risk around fishing. Chum, scent, leaders, and catch-loss rules all affect that danger.

Sharks

Supported sharks can react to fishing activity and scent.

Chum

Chum can raise scent in the water. More scent can mean more shark attention.

Catch loss

A shark threat can put a catch at risk. Leaders can reduce the chance of losing that catch.

Optional spawning

Some shark spawning behavior depends on installed mods and server settings.
