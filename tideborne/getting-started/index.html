Tideborne/Getting Started
Start here

Catch a fish. Read it. Keep it.

Tide 2 is the base fishing mod. Tideborne adds special fish traits, FishScore, the Angler's Satchel, and records.

What you need

Tideborne 2.0.1 is made for Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric. Tide 2 is required.

1. Fish with Tide 2

Cast your rod. Wait for a bite. Use the Tide 2 fishing minigame to reel the fish in.

Catch the fish

Keep the moving bar in the catch area.

Change your rod

Use hooks, lines, and bobbers at the Angling Table.

2. Read the fish

Every caught fish can have a size and four trait groups.

Body Type

Normal, Giant, or Dwarf.

Condition

Normal, Scarred, or Parasite-Ridden.

Pigmentation

Normal, Albino, or Iridescent.

Quality

A very strong fish may become a Perfect Specimen.

3. Check FishScore

FishScore puts the full catch on a 1–3000 scale. A higher score means a stronger trophy.

4. Keep important fish

Use the Angler's Satchel to store, protect, and sort fish.

5. Track your progress

Use the Journal for discoveries and records. Use the Fish Wiki when you need fish information.
