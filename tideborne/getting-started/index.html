Tideborne/Getting Started
Start here

From your first cast to your first trophy.

Tide provides the core fishing experience. Tideborne builds a persistent specimen, progression, record and collection layer around the fish you catch.

The short versionFish normally with Tide. Tideborne takes over the specimen side: unusual traits, FishScore, records, the Angler's Satchel and related progression.

What you need

Tideborne 2.0.1 targets Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric and builds on Tide 2. The exact dependency/version reference lives with Compatibility as this site expands.

1. Learn Tide's catch loop

Use Tide's fishing flow: equip a compatible rod, cast, wait for a bite and complete the catch interaction. Tideborne does not replace that familiar loop—it adds meaning to the resulting specimen.

2. Understand rod customization

At Tide's Angling Table, compatible rods can carry line, bobber and hook accessories. Tideborne builds its gear interactions on top of that ownership model.

Angling Table GUI

Official Tide wiki screenshot of the rod/accessory layout.

3. Read the specimen

After species selection and the natural specimen foundation are established, Tideborne can finalize four independent specimen axes: Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation and Quality. One unusual trait does not consume another trait category.

Body Type

Normal, Giant or Dwarf.

Condition

Physical condition independent from body and color.

Pigmentation

Rare pigmentation states can stack with other traits.

Quality

Perfect Specimen follows its own high-percentile curve.

4. Check FishScore

FishScore compresses species value, specimen percentile and compatible trait points into the canonical 1–3000 scale. It is designed for comparing trophies without erasing the traits that made them interesting.

5. Keep what matters

The Angler's Satchel is the specimen-management hub. It stores catches, supports protection and sorting, grows through upgrades, and presents records without changing the underlying specimen identity.

6. Build a fishing history

Tide already provides a Fishing Journal for discovery and fish information. Tideborne extends the record idea with canonical specimen data, personal/team records and Top Fish.
