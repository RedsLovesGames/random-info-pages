Settings

Change Tideborne without hunting through files.

The in-game settings screen groups options by what they change.

Main groups

Fishing

Catching, gear, bait, bobbers, and balance.

Satchel

Behavior, sorting, and upgrades.

Journal & Teams

Journal, records, teams, and multiplayer options.

Sharks & Ecosystem

Sharks, chum, catch loss, and optional spawning.

Client & HUD

Things shown only on your screen.

Advanced

Low-level tuning and less common settings.

Client or server?

Server settings

Change gameplay for everyone on that server.

Client settings

Change only what you see on your own screen.

Old configsOlder Tideborne config files are handled for compatibility. You normally do not need to edit them by hand.
