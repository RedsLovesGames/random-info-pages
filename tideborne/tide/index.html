Tideborne/Tide 2 Foundations
The base mod

Tide 2 comes first.

Tide 2 is the base mod for Tideborne. Tide 2 adds the main fishing system. Tideborne adds more features on top of it.

Simple meaningTide 2 gives you fishing, fish, the catch minigame, rod parts, bait, the Angling Table and the Fishing Journal. Tideborne adds special fish traits, FishScore, the Angler's Satchel, records, teams, sharks and more.
Gameplay source: this page follows the current Tide 2 repository. Older Tide wiki images are still used because they are useful and look good. If an old image or old wiki page shows different gameplay, the current Tide 2 game behavior wins.
Tide 2 makes the fishing world. Tideborne gives each catch more value after you catch it.

Start with Tide 2

You do not need to learn Tideborne first. Learn the Tide 2 fishing loop first.

1. Get a fishing rod

Use a normal Minecraft rod or a rod added by Tide 2.

2. Cast the line

Hold right-click. Release it to cast.

3. Wait for a fish

A fish will come to the hook. Bait can help.

4. Catch the fish

Play Tide 2's fishing minigame. Keep the moving bar in the good area.

Fishing rods

Tide 2 works with the normal fishing rod and its own fishing rods.

The fishing minigame

Every fish can start a small catch game. A bar moves on the screen. Right-click when the bar is inside the colored area.

Some fish are easy. Some fish move faster or fight harder.

Watch the bar

Try to keep the moving bar inside the colored catch area.

Hard fish move more

Different fish can have different movement and strength.

Tideborne adds moreA very good catch can also matter to Tideborne. Tideborne can use your catch result for systems such as Perfect Catch.

Use bait

Bait can help you fish faster. Some bait can also give a special bonus.

You can read the item information in the game to see what a bait does.

Customize your rod

Tide 2 adds the Angling Table. Use it to change a fishing rod.

You can add three kinds of parts: hooks, lines and bobbers.

Angling Table

Place this block in the world. Use it to change your rod.

Rod parts

Choose a hook, line and bobber. You can mix them in different ways.

Hooks, lines and bobbers

Hooks

Hooks can give useful fishing effects.

Lines

Lines can change how fishing works or make some catches easier.

Bobbers

Bobbers change how the bobber looks. Tideborne can also show or use extra bobber effects when those effects exist.

Special fishing

Some Tide 2 gear can unlock unusual fishing, such as lava or void fishing.

The Fishing Journal

Tide 2 has a Fishing Journal. It updates when you catch fish.

Use it to see your progress, large catches, fish habitats and other fish information.

Fishing Journal

See fish you have found and your collection progress.

Fish profile

Open a fish page to learn more about that fish.

You can also find fishy notes in crates. These notes can give hints about where to find a fish.

More than fish

What Tideborne adds

After Tide 2 gives you a fish, Tideborne can make that single catch special.

Traits

A fish can have special features. Examples are Giant, Dwarf or special color states.

FishScore

A number from 1 to 3000. It helps you compare fish.

Angler's Satchel

A place to keep, protect, sort and manage your fish.

Records and teams

Save good catches. Compare them with other players.
