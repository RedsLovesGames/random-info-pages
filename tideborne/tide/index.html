Tideborne/Tide Foundations
The game underneath Tideborne

Know Tide, then go deeper.

Tideborne is built on top of Tide's fishing loop, equipment system and Journal. This page brings the useful general-wiki material into the Tideborne site so you do not have to bounce between documentation just to understand the base systems.

Visual source: renders, screenshots and sprite sheets below come from Lightning-64's official Tide wiki repository. Some of that wiki documents older Tide behavior, so current Tideborne mechanics/reference pages take priority whenever gameplay details differ.
Tide's fishing world is the foundation. Tideborne adds persistent specimen identity, traits, FishScore, storage, records and progression around the catches Tide produces.

The base fishing loop

At the player level the flow is simple: use a compatible rod, cast, wait for a bite, reel in, and complete Tide's catch interaction. Tideborne does not replace that loop; it takes the resulting catch and gives the specimen long-term meaning.

The Angling Table

Tide's Angling Table is the center of rod customization. The official wiki documents a rod input/output area plus accessory slots for line, bobber and hook. Tideborne keeps that equipment language and adds its own readable presentation and gameplay integrations around it.
