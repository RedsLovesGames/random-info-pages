04 / DOCUMENTATION

How to read this

Each cell needs evidence

Attach the speaker's precise words, a date, and a source URL. Avoid confusing a question, repost, joke, hypothetical, or reporting of a claim with endorsement. Differentiate confirmed direct statements from secondary reporting. Review changes over time.

Records stored here are browser-local via localStorage. They will not sync between devices, and are not moderated. Use the JSON export for backup. Published entries are shipped with the page; your edits stay local. Remove a local override to reveal the published record.

Two distinct kinds of data

The 17 original aggregates are attributed to the source and cover its selected 40 people. The much larger roster and topic catalog are a scaffold for further research. Published evidence includes a source, evidence level, date where established, and notes. Unknown pairings remain blank. No fabricated quotations, assumed private beliefs, or sample votes.

Some entries describe documented historical programs rather than unsupported conspiracy theories. Topic inclusion is for comparative analysis, not endorsement. For any disputed claim, consult independent primary records and established reporting.
