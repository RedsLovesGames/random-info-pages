Methodology & reporting limits

Snapshot: information checked the morning of November 5, 2026 (Eastern time). This is a manually maintained research snapshot, not a live election feed. Counts may change as elections are called, canvassed, or recounted.

Definitions: U.S. House and Senate flips mean Republican-held seats won by Democrats. A state legislative chamber gain means a newly obtained Democratic majority; Minnesota's state House moved from a tie to a Democratic majority. A new trifecta requires Democratic control of the governorship and both chambers, and is not synonymous with a governor flip.

House count warning: Ballotpedia reported 30 flipped House districts as of its November 5 morning analysis; the individual district ledger here contains 28 identifiable called flips shown by Polling Desk in the consulted snapshot. These providers' update schedules and seat-change classifications differ. Do not mistake the 28 named rows for all 30 reported by Ballotpedia. State shading reflects only those 28 named races.

Uncalled races: Arizona Senate and Georgia House are treated as pending control changes based on NCSL's November 4 analysis, not as confirmed flips. This map does not imply other states' contests were fully called. For 2026 House races, redistricting in several states means comparing district identifiers against 2024 may not compare identical boundaries.

Map geometry: U.S. state outlines reused from the Random Info Pages Infrastructure Atlas, including illustrative insets for Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico. State-level geography is not a congressional-district map.
