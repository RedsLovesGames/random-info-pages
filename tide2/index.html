Fishing System 2.0

Same fishing game. Better reasons to keep casting.

The minigame stays. The frustrating parts underneath it get rebuilt: Luck becomes predictable, rare traits stop being a second impossible lottery, Giant and Dwarf stop needing absurd tail rolls, and trophy fish finally have a score you can understand.

Keep Tide's minigameMake rare fish worth huntingLet special traits stack

01

What actually changes

No implementation-speak. These are the differences a player should notice.

Fishing Luck

“Luck” finally means something specific.

BeforeLuck could touch several loot decisions.It was hard to know what +15 actually bought you.
NowFishing Luck only pushes the fish pool toward rarer species.It does not secretly change the overall fish-catch chance.
Player impact: build for rare fish and know exactly what the stat is doing.
Traits

Special fish can be special in more than one way.

BeforeSpecial states competed more directly.Making one thing easier could distort the rarity of another.
NowBody, condition, pigmentation and specimen quality are separate axes.A Giant Iridescent Perfect fish is a real combination, not a mutually exclusive choice.
Player impact: single traits can be huntable while stacked trophy fish stay genuinely rare.
Rare species

Rare fish no longer multiply the mutation grind as brutally.

BeforeRare species × rare trait could become a lottery on top of a lottery.
NowHigher-rarity species get better trait odds once you actually catch them.
5★ 2.40×

trait-event multiplier before Trait Luck is applied.

Size

One fish gets one natural percentile.

BeforePercentile approximation and extreme-tail gates made size logic harder to follow.
NowNatural percentile is generated once. Giant or Dwarf changes physical length, not the fish's original roll.
Player impact: a 99th-percentile fish is actually a 99th-percentile fish, not a value later rewritten by another mechanic.
Perfect Catch

Skill improves the trophy, not the past.

BeforeOlder compatibility behavior could influence specimen outcomes after the fight.
NowPerfect Catch gives temporary Trait Luck, boosts Body Type chance and helps Perfect Specimen.It never rerolls natural size.
Player impact: playing well matters, but it cannot magically turn a naturally average fish into a naturally huge one.
Fight feel

Keep the minigame. Fix the dead zones.

The marker, catch region, behavior pattern and center-zone Perfect Catch stay. The numbers feeding them get normalized so nearby Strength and Speed values do not collapse into the same feel.

FishScore

The score tells you why the fish is good.

Rarity + percentile + traits produce a raw score. That raw score is then mapped linearly to the public 1–3000 scale. World records are separate, so your fish does not lose points because somebody else catches a bigger one later.

Player impact: trophy value belongs to the specimen itself.

05

Size becomes a specimen story you can follow

Natural percentile is rolled once, then physical modifiers change the actual length. Those are two different facts and the new system keeps them separate.

Natural size

A percentile is permanent.

If the fish naturally rolls P97, that is its natural P97 roll. Giant or Dwarf can change its final physical length, but they do not reroll the natural specimen underneath it.

BeforeApproximation + extreme-tail logic.
NowDirect distribution math + one saved percentile.

08

What a catch looks like now

The 15 implementation steps are easier to understand as five player-facing phases.

Phase 1Find the fishBuild the eligible pool, apply Fishing Luck, choose a species.
Phase 2Build the specimenRoll natural size, then Body Type and physical length.
Phase 3Fight itNormalize Strength and Tempo, apply size/body/gear modifiers, play Tide's minigame.
Phase 4Reward skillPerfect Catch affects trait pressure and Perfect Specimen, never natural size.
Phase 5Make it a trophyRoll Condition and Pigmentation, score the fish, update Satchel and records.

Exact math, if you want it

Kept out of the main comparison so the page reads like a game-system explanation instead of an implementation document.

Fishing Luck formula
Encounter weight W, Fishing Luck L, rarity coefficient CR.W' = W × (1 + C_R × √max(L, 0)) Coefficients: 1★ 0.00 · 2★ 0.08 · 3★ 0.16 · 4★ 0.24 · 5★ 0.32
Tempo and size-fight formulas
Tempo V = 0.04 + 0.085 × ln(1 + s) / ln(3.2) X = (P - 50) / 50 Strength multiplier = 1 + 0.12X Tempo multiplier = 1 - 0.06X
Trait probability formula
P' = 1 - (1 - P)^(1 + T / 10) Rarity compensation: 1★ 1.00× · 2★ 1.15× · 3★ 1.40× · 4★ 1.80× · 5★ 2.40×
Perfect Specimen base curve
Below P95 = 0%. P95 = 2%. P97.5 = 8%. P99 = 25%. P99.9+ = 60%. The curve interpolates smoothly, then Trait Luck and Perfect Catch are applied.
Raw FishScore formula
RawScore = SpeciesValue + 3 × finalPercentile + TraitValue Species: 1★ 50 · 2★ 100 · 3★ 175 · 4★ 250 · 5★ 350 Traits: Scarred +20 · Parasite +35 · Giant/Dwarf +40 · Albino +70 · Iridescent +100 · Perfect +100
