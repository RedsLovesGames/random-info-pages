Player guide

Catch rare fish. Build stranger trophies.

Fishing System 2.0 separates which species you hook from what makes that specimen special. Fishing Luck hunts rarer species. Trait Luck hunts unusual specimens. Skill, percentile, rarity and per-species Momentum all matter without making one rare trait block another.

Fishing Luck = species selectionTrait Luck = specimen traitsFishScore = 1 to 3000

The core change

Old problemRare species and rare traits could become a lottery stacked on another lottery.

Special states also competed too directly.

→
Fishing 2.0Species rarity and specimen rarity are separate levers.

Traits get independent rolls and rare species receive trait compensation.

01

One catch, in player terms

Perfect Catch is known before the game settles the final trait odds. That means skill can help the finished specimen without rerolling the fish you already hooked.

1Species selectedYour eligible fish pool is built and Fishing Luck changes the relative species weights.
2Natural specimen establishedThe fish gets its natural size information and one natural percentile roll.
3Fight the fishStrength, Tempo, size, body and gear shape Tide's familiar catch minigame.
4Perfect Catch knownYour center-zone skill result is locked in before the final trait chances are settled.
5Final trait odds resolveRarity, Trait Luck, Momentum and Perfect Catch apply where their rules say they should.
6Specimen finalizedBody Type, Condition, Pigmentation, Quality, size and specimen percentile become final.
7FishScore calculatedSpecies value, specimen percentile and compatible trait points produce one score.
8The trophy persistsThe same specimen identity follows the fish through items, entities, buckets, displays and journals.
Perfect Catch does not reroll species or natural percentile. It adds +10 temporary Trait Luck, multiplies the Body Type event chance by 1.25, and improves the Perfect Specimen quality roll through its dedicated quality reward.
02

Fishing Luck only chooses species

It never directly makes a fish Giant, Albino, Iridescent, Scarred or Perfect. It changes relative selection weight, then the whole eligible pool is normalized back into actual probabilities.

Relative weight multiplier

Weight is not probability

A 2.24× weight does not mean a 2.24× final catch chance.

Every eligible fish competes in the same normalized pool. Biome, water type, time, weather, dimensions and other eligibility rules decide which fish are even competing.

Fishing Luck: species selection. Trait Luck: specimen traits. They do not substitute for each other.

05

Trait Momentum is per player, per species

Long normal streaks quietly improve Trait Luck for that one species. It persists with the player, caps at 15, and is not stored on a fish item.

Bass7
Tuna0
Dragon Fish13
Each species keeps its own value for this player.

A fully normal specimen adds exactly +1 Momentum for that species, up to 15. Any notable result on Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation or Quality resets that species' Momentum to 0. Other species are untouched.

Compatibility fish

Supported fish keep real species character

Compatibility fish are adapted into Fishing 2.0 using real species properties such as rarity, encounter weight, environment restrictions, size distribution, Strength, Tempo and behavior. They are not flattened into one generic external-fish profile.

What happens to existing fish?

Your old trophies should keep their identity.

Legacy Tideborne fish are migrated into the new specimen system rather than being randomized from scratch. Known physical size, percentile and catch information are preserved whenever they can be recovered. Scores are recalculated using the V2 formula so old and new equivalent specimens compare fairly.

Legacy Giant → Giant Body Type
Legacy Dwarf → Dwarf Body Type
Scarred → Scarred Condition
Parasite → Parasite-Ridden
Albino → Albino Pigmentation
Iridescent → Iridescent Pigmentation
Perfect → Perfect Specimen
The point: your existing trophy fish should remain recognizable specimens instead of becoming random new fish.
One score system

Migration preserves the fish, not obsolete score numbers.

Old FishScore values are recalculated with the V2 species + percentile + trait formula. Records remain separate from the intrinsic FishScore, so another player catching a new record does not change your specimen's score.

The main guide stays player-facing. These are the frozen formulas behind the interactives.
