exact numbers

A compact readable reference for the constants used by the player guide. This page mirrors the authoritative Tideborne Fishing System 2.0 mechanics currently used by the guide without linking players to an unavailable project document.

01

Species selection

Fishing Luck changes eligible fish selection weight only.

W' = W × (1 + C × √max(L, 0)) Rarity coefficients 1★ 0.00 2★ 0.08 3★ 0.16 4★ 0.24 5★ 0.32

The resulting weights are normalized against every other eligible fish, so weight multiplier is not the same thing as final catch probability.

02

Standard specimen traits

Body Type and Condition start at 5%. Pigmentation starts at 1.5%. Body Type, Condition and Pigmentation use rarity compensation, then Trait Luck.

Trait Luck P' = 1 - (1 - P)^(1 + T / 10) Rarity compensation 1★ ×1.00 2★ ×1.15 3★ ×1.40 4★ ×1.80 5★ ×2.40

On a Perfect Catch, Body Type gets an additional ×1.25 event multiplier before rarity compensation and Trait Luck. Perfect Catch also contributes +10 temporary Trait Luck.

03

Body Type subtype

giantProbability = 0.25 + 0.50 × (percentile / 100) dwarfProbability = 1 - giantProbability

This subtype calculation happens only if the Body Type event triggers. Giant remains possible at the low end and Dwarf remains possible at the high end.

05

Trait Momentum

Momentum is persistent per player and per species, with a cap of 15. A fully normal catch adds +1 for that species. A notable Body Type, Condition, Pigmentation or Quality result resets that species' Momentum to 0. Momentum contributes temporary Trait Luck to future catches of that species.

07

Perfect Catch

Perfect Catch uses Tide's center-zone skill check. It does not reroll species or natural percentile. It contributes +10 temporary Trait Luck, multiplies the Body Type event chance by ×1.25, and gives Perfect Specimen its dedicated second-opportunity quality reward.
