Sources and methodology

Open the underlying records

Campaign finance: FEC candidate totals, Schedule A employer groupings, and disclosed PAC / political committee contribution transactions.

Service-era totals: summed two-year FEC cycles beginning with the election cycle leading into the member's first term in the congressional roster.

Donor employers: employers reported by itemized individual contributors. This does not mean the employer itself contributed the displayed amount.

Coverage: smaller unitemized contributions cannot be assigned to named donors or employers. FEC totals can change when committees file amendments.
