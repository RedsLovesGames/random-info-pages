# A Day in Messages

This static GitHub Pages exhibit uses 18 independently sourced historical milestones and covers the stated 24-hour span (03:00 EDT September 11 through 03:00 EDT September 12, 2001) of the WikiLeaks pager archive.

**Data status:** Raw pager traffic was **not** included. The page deliberately shows no message-volume bar heights until a complete and validated aggregate is available. No private pager traffic is published. This is a verified historical chronology, not a completed statistical analysis of the original intercept dataset.

Original source: https://911.wikileaks.org/files/index.html

Research starter scripts, privacy requirements and source references are in the research ZIP delivered in the originating chat.
